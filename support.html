Instrument Assistance

Hungry for Music

Support offered: Donated instruments for children facing financial barriers to music participation.

Who can apply: Eligible Title One public-school staff, after-school and summer music-program directors, social workers, and school counselors.

Service area: United States; international programs require a U.S.-based sponsor

Families should request help through an eligible teacher, program director, social worker, or school counselor. Check the current application dates before applying.

Music Program Funding

Classics for Kids Foundation

Support offered: Matching grants to purchase stringed instruments for youth music programs.

Who can apply: Schools and nonprofit organizations serving kindergarten through grade 12. Nonprofit-status documentation is required; individuals cannot apply.

Service area: United States

Matching funds are required. The foundation covers no more than 50% of the total instrument cost. Review the current application requirements and quarterly deadlines before applying.
