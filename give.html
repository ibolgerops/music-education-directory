Donate Instruments

Hungry for Music

Accepts: Playable instruments and instruments needing repair. Upright pianos and organs are excluded.

Donation area: Drop-off and pickup options depend on location and volunteer availability. Shipping instructions are provided by the organization.

Complete the organization's instrument donation form to arrange delivery, drop-off, or possible pickup.

Contribute Funds

Classics for Kids Foundation

Funds support: Matching grants that help youth music programs purchase stringed instruments.

Communities served: School and community youth music programs across the United States.

Giving options include one-time donations, recurring donations, and employer matching gifts. Visit the foundation's website for details.
